Callisto artwork credits

Callisto surface map: the Voyager / Galileo SSI global mosaic (1 km) by the USGS Astrogeology Science Center, public domain.

The mosaic was resampled to a 4K brightness map, its no-data gaps were filled with terrain from the opposite hemisphere, and its contrast was balanced for print. The renderer maps the image onto a rotating sphere, enhances terrain contrast and lighting, and renders it as ASCII artwork.
